I had finally included the `search` action and added secret doors to the main generator (now called "dungeon") but secret door placement sucks. I think that most of the people I really look up to in the roguelike space, like Loren Schmidt, Brian Bucklew or Brian Walker, have fantastic minds for procgen and I have all of these design opinions about it but I'm somewhat helpless in implementing them. I think it's pretty obvious that the sophisticated "Palladian" generator type I added received a pretty hefty amount of help from mephistopheles, which has likely tainted me forever. Also my ideas about using WFC may not really pan out for something as complicated as a dungeon generator. It would have to be some sort of combination of WFC and something more sophisticated. I think all I can do for now is try to keep architect from becoming such an ugly hairball that no one else wants to touch it and find help from a human. Anyway, I now have a lot of silly secret door placement, but I decided to move on to some important UI additions that I've been looking forward to.

### Info windows

We already had limited info windows for the player and item info windows that are just verbs, now we have really packed info windows for important actors (anything with a description field or sentient true) and items. What's more is we have new interaction conventions for getting info on something out in the environment (keyboard or mouse driven) and a little minimal zoom animation that is reusable to other things like taunts (I was aiming for early GUI window zooms that were barely there). Info windows have got be back into the weeds with player character epistemology, which I talked about at length [here](2026-08-26-epistemology.md). Namely, there was a leak at first where while hallucinating you still got detailed info about actors even though the actors tiles were scrambled. Since I hadn't actually gotten to masking actor names in the html sidebar actors-list, I could have probably been lazy and left this, with the thinking that maybe the player character is on top of things, is an old hand at tripping (living in a fungal world full of ergot-tainted grain and weird encounters with gods, ghosts, witches and demons) and has held on to an internal knowledge of what things were before they started melting down into the symbol-set that makes up the world. But, I do think that's laziness on my part. The handicap of the hallucination state is in not knowing what stuff is other than maybe the items in your inventory. So, I fixed info windows with a hallucation version, and next I will fix the sidebar actors list. I also need to get item tiles in the field to randomize along with actor tiles.
