Before I wire up the editors, I'd like to try to wrangle them a bit.

Usually the way I make things is to watch when an organizational structure gets overburdened and then look for potential structures inside it and then split those out into their own containers/surfaces. Right now the item editor is hurting because items do such wildly different things.

I am thinking of two possible solutions, and I may do both.. the first is, are item types so different from one another that they should have their own editor views? Or do they share so many common features that this would just duplicate a bunch of stuff for minor differences.

The second- I am looking at the extreme complexity of items like staffs- all the different things that spells can do, and they are all hard-coded. Should there be another data type? spells.json, magic.json? that includes an effect selection but also some of the other things like a use verb and targeting etc?

I think it's not worthwhile to have different views, most items aren't typed, most types share attributes. Reorganizing what's still there after spells get moved to their own data type and editor will probably clean up a lot.
